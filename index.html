Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

Dispositor is a local-first browser workspace organizer. It helps users create and organize workspaces, notes, tasks, tabs, bookmarks, and related organizational data in the browser.

2. Local data storage

User-created workspaces and workspace content are stored locally on the user's device. This includes notes, tasks, organizational data, saved tabs or sessions, and other content created in Dispositor.

Dispositor does not upload or synchronize user-created workspace content to an external server or cloud service. Local storage may use browser storage and a local database maintained by the extension.

3. Authentication and account information

Authentication is available to provide access to account-related and additional product capabilities. Signing in is optional and is handled through a separate OAuth authorization flow.

During authentication, Dispositor processes authentication-related information needed to establish and maintain the account session. This may include account identifiers, email address, display name or profile information, provider information, authorization codes, OAuth state, PKCE values, access tokens, and refresh tokens. In particular, Google OAuth may provide account profile and authentication information to the authentication flow.

The flow uses Supabase Auth as authentication infrastructure and a first-party Dispositor authentication page hosted on Vercel to present provider consent and account selection. Google may process sign-in and account information when Google authentication is used. Supabase and the relevant authentication providers may receive or process authentication and session information needed to operate the sign-in process.

Authentication session details are retained in browser storage for as long as the extension session remains available or until they are removed by sign-out, expiration, revocation, or browser storage cleanup. Transient OAuth flow values are kept only as needed to complete sign-in.

4. Browser permissions

  • offscreen: allows Dispositor to run a local background page that hosts the extension's SQLite storage functionality in Chrome.
  • sessions: declares browser-session functionality used for Dispositor's session-oriented features. This permission does not mean that Dispositor transmits browsing history or workspace content to external servers.
  • storage: allows Dispositor to save settings, local workspace data, account information, and authentication-session state in browser-managed storage.
  • tabs: allows Dispositor to read currently open-tab titles and URLs when needed for tab organization and session features, and to open a tab for extension navigation or account actions.

Dispositor does not use these permissions to upload or synchronize local workspace content to external servers.

5. Data sharing

Dispositor does not sell user data. It does not upload or synchronize user-created workspace content to external servers.

Authentication necessarily involves third-party processing. Google and Supabase, and the hosting provider for the first-party authentication page where applicable, may process authentication or session information as part of providing account access and OAuth services. Their handling is governed by their own privacy terms and applicable configuration.

6. Data security and retention

Workspace data is stored locally by the extension. Authentication information is stored in browser storage, including persistent storage for an extension-owned session and transient browser storage for in-progress authentication. Signing out attempts to revoke the active authentication session and removes the local session and pending flow values.

Dispositor uses HTTPS for its authentication network connections and browser storage managed by the user's browser. No software can guarantee absolute security, and security ultimately depends on the user's browser and device. This policy does not create a longer or shorter local-retention promise than the user's control, browser behavior, or authentication-provider session handling.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Dispositor develops or as authentication providers, browser capabilities, or applicable requirements change. When updated, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised.
